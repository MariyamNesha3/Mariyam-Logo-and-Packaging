Brand Story

The name Ocellaris is inspired by Amphiprion ocellaris, commonly known as the ocellaris clownfish, a vibrant marine species found in tropical waters. This connection to marine life forms the foundation of the brand’s visual identity, bringing together the freshness of water and the colourful beauty of the ocean. Shades of blue, flowing typography, and underwater illustrations translate this inspiration into distinctive packaging that feels refreshing, lively, and memorable.

Brand Type

Packaged Drinking Water | FMCG

Design Approach

Inspired by the ocean’s natural colours and movement, the packaging uses layered blues, aquatic illustrations, and bold typography to create a striking underwater experience. The design balances visual storytelling with clear product information, helping the brand stand out on the shelf.
